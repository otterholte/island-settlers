Island Settlers — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Island Settlers does not collect, store or share personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind. This policy covers both the Android app (com.otterholte.islandsettlersapp) and the web version at otterholte.github.io/island-settlers.

What the app stores on your device

The game keeps a small amount of information in your device's local storage so it can remember your preferences between sessions:

  • the display name you type before playing with friends;
  • your settings — sound, difficulty, button side, and similar;
  • which tutorial steps you have already seen;
  • a random identifier the game generates for itself so it can tell your device apart from another player's inside a multiplayer room.

All of this stays on your device. None of it is transmitted anywhere except as described under multiplayer below, none of it is backed up to the cloud by the app, and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. The random identifier is not an advertising ID, is not linked to you, and is not shared with anyone.

Playing on your own

Single-player matches and the tutorial run entirely on your device. The app's assets are bundled inside it, so the game does not need a network connection at all and makes no network requests while you play alone.

Playing with friends (multiplayer)

If — and only if — you choose Play with friends, the game opens a connection to the match server at island-settlers-production.up.railway.app. While you are in a room, the following is sent to that server so the other players in your room can see it:

  • the display name you typed;
  • the five-character room code;
  • your moves in the match — where your settler is, what you gather, build and trade.

The server holds this in memory only, for the length of the match. There is no database, no user account and no log of past games; when the room closes or the server restarts, everything about your session is gone. Your name is whatever you typed and is not verified, connected to any identity, or used for anything other than labelling your seat on the other players' screens. As with any internet connection, your device's IP address is visible to the server while connected; it is used to carry the connection and is not recorded.

Please do not use your real full name, an email address, or any other personal detail as your display name — other players in the room will see it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.
  • No in-app purchases and no payment processing.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone, location, or calendar. The app requests exactly two Android permissions: INTERNET, used only for multiplayer, and VIBRATE, used for haptic feedback.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, because there is no data to sell or share.

Children

Island Settlers is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 in particular, but it is safe for them: it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Because the multiplayer mode lets players choose a display name that other players in the same room can see, parents may prefer that younger children play the single-player game, or use a nickname.

Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or retained, there is nothing to request access to, correct, export or delete. Removing everything the game has ever stored about you is done by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage on your device. If you have a question about this, write to the address below and it will be answered.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts doing something this page does not describe, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
